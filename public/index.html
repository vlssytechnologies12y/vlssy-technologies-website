Questions, answered

Start with
curiosity.

A good project starts with an honest conversation about what is working, what is not and what comes next.

What LMS solutions do you provide?

We provide Moodle implementation, customization, migration, integrations, reporting, SCORM/H5P, assessments, certification and ongoing maintenance.

Do you customize Moodle?

Yes. We can tailor the user experience, workflows, roles, reports and integrations around your learning model.

Can you integrate an existing LMS with other systems?

Yes. We connect LMS platforms with ERP, HR, CRM, identity and other operational systems through APIs and reliable data flows.

Do you provide ongoing LMS support?

Yes. Support and AMC can include monitoring, updates, troubleshooting, backups and continuous improvements.

Can you develop custom software?

Yes. We build business applications, web applications, dashboards and workflow systems for specific operational needs.

Can you integrate AI into an existing application?

Yes. We assess the use case, data and workflow, then design a practical AI integration with appropriate safeguards.

Do you provide cloud/server management?

Yes. Services include deployment, VPS and server management, backups, monitoring, hardening and performance optimization.

Do you provide AMC/support?

Yes. We offer ongoing technical support and AMC arrangements tailored to the systems and service levels you need.
